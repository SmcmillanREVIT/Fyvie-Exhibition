About the Exhibition

Explore over 800 years of history through detailed architectural elevations of the Fyvie Castle estate buildings. Each piece highlights the unique heritage and character of structures such as the Racquet Court, Game Larder, Butler House, and more. The exhibition showcases these visuals both as historical records and modern art forms.
